# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be harassment-free for everyone, regardless of
experience level, background, identity, or how they use the software.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume competence. Ask what someone is trying to achieve before assuming they are wrong.
- Critique code and decisions, not people.
- Give reproduction steps when reporting a problem; accept them when receiving one.
- Respect a maintainer's decision to decline a change, and ask for the reasoning if it is unclear.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, insults, or sustained disruption of discussion.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission.
- Dismissing reports of bad behaviour.

## Scope

Applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any space where someone is
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainer via the contact on the GitHub profile. Reports are handled
privately. Maintainers may warn, remove content, or ban, depending on severity and repetition.
